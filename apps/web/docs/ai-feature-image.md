## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a single landscape 4:3 editorial photograph for the subject page 'Artificiell intelligens' on a Swedish publication. The subject must be unmistakably a conversational AI interface, photographed as a physical screen, not a UI mockup. Tight, slightly oblique close-up of an ordinary used laptop screen and a little of its keyboard on an unremarkable library table beside a window on an overcast afternoon. No person. The screen occupies about two thirds of the composition and is the focal point. Simple contemporary neutral AI chat interface, warm off-white screen background, charcoal text, no corporate brand, no colorful graphics. At the top of the interface the single clear heading is exactly 'AI-assistent'. One user message reads exactly 'Vad menas med artificiell intelligens?' Beneath it a modest response beginning 'Artificiell intelligens' with several small text lines falling out of focus; a discreet text input strip at the bottom. Keep it recognizably a text assistant rather than a messaging app. No people avatars. Frame the laptop at a candid off-axis angle, a little cropped on the left, with quiet grey window reflection across one corner of the dark screen bezel and very slight glare over some of the interface. Some finger smudges on the bezel, one worn keyboard key, subtle screen pixel texture; believable everyday imperfections, no exaggerated dirt. Background: only an out-of-focus slice of tall library windows and vague grey-green trees outside. No arranged props, no book, no cup, no desk lamp, no ornaments, no staged study. Soft ordinary Nordic daylight, low saturation, warm grey and weathered brown with restrained blue-grey reflections, documentary 35mm film texture, natural exposure, moderate depth of field, no cinematic rim light, no golden hour, no dramatic spotlight, no HDR, no glossy advertising style. The image must read as a quiet observational photograph, visually compatible with restrained editorial photographs of people and places. Keep the AI heading, user query and response visible in a centered 3:2 crop. No robot, no horse, no brain icon, no circuit diagram, no holograms, no sci-fi, no floating symbols, no watermarks, no typography outside the physical screen.

## Essay series covers (2026-11-07)

Five of the seven covers, made in August, were one picture: a person alone at a desk by a

what was asked for was a frame a master photographer had considered. What held:

- **One photographic idea a frame, and no idea twice in a set.** Hjärtats anatomi: the heart
  model seen through vitrine glass with the winter window reflected over it (the heart as a
  mirror). Maskinen och människan: straight down onto three
  rows of identical trays of envelopes, the one clerk lifting an envelope out. Valet 2026: a level, frontal row of voting screens, only the voters' feet
  showing, a woman's long skirt among them. Tungan och sanningen: a long lens on the
  minaret's loudspeaker in snow, the church spire soft behind it. Strindbergs kris: the
  study through two open double doors, frame within frame.
- **Vary the grammar across the set:** still life, overhead pattern, deadpan typology,
  long-lens detail, interior in depth. (Superseded the same day for everything but these
  covers: see below. The fault was one scene repeated, not the figure or the lamp.)
- **Decide every edge, as the photographer would.** The first mail hall was shot steeply
  down through a wide lens: the rows splayed, the edges cut trays at random and the clerk
  stood against one side; the user: »the way that is cut or cropped does not look like the
  thinking of a master photographer«. The placed one is a true overhead with the rows
  parallel to the frame, cropped by measurement (a brightness profile gives the rows and the
  tray pitch): both side edges on a tray divider, equal floor above the top row and below
  the bottom rail, the clerk on the lower-right third. Ask Codex for the geometry, then
  crop for the edges yourself; it never lands them.
- **Judge at card size.** Two quiet towers on a snow field were the better photograph and
  vanished at 373 px; a still life of the alchemy props looked staged.
- The prompt opened »Imagine it was made by a master photographer for a museum exhibition:
  one considered idea, a deliberate vantage point, precise geometry and clean edges, light
  used as part of the subject, stillness, and a meaning that rewards a second look …« and
  kept this file's closing list (soft northern daylight, no golden hour, no lamp glow, no
  HDR, no readable text, no woman's hair). Ask for landscape outright: one of ten came back

### What the user wants: emotion before concept (2026-11-07)

The same day, the method above was applied to 18 essay and Fördjupning pictures on the
front page and hubs: conceptual, deadpan frames in flat daylight, no figure from behind,
no lamp glow. The user kept 15 of the originals (»the current is the clear winner since
it is much more intelligent and emotional and a clear master piece«) and called the
proposals »waaay to boring, lack emotion and a sense of artistic style«. What the kept
pictures share is the brief to start from:

- **A human presence carrying a feeling**: devotion, tenderness, longing, grief, awe. A
  figure from behind, in profile, a silhouette or a pair of hands is welcome (the modesty
  rules hold).
- **Light as drama**: one warm source in darkness, dusk, a night sky, weather. The
  »no golden hour, no lamp glow« list in the prompt above belongs to that one laptop
  picture; do not apply it as a ban.
- **A story caught in a moment**, not a symbol to decode. Concept still lifes (a shoe
  shelf, a scorch mark from above, a miswak beside a toothbrush) read as clever and cold.
- The series-cover fault was five pictures of one scene side by side. Vary the scene
  across a set; keep the emotional register.

The three proposals taken were the warmest: a threshold stone worn hollow with old clogs
beside it, a fig tree in a snowy window, spent grave lanterns the morning after All Saints.
