# Essay title review

Reviewed the archive as a set and read the selected essays before revising their titles. Repeated cryptic metaphors, possessives, and paired nouns were obscuring the differences between subjects. The replacements vary in rhythm and construction, while offering a concrete subject, recognizable tension, or question the essay addresses. Literary titles elsewhere remain to preserve variety.

Only title frontmatter changes; URLs, essay bodies, descriptions, images, and recordings remain intact.

| Previous title | Revised title | Editorial reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Det han letade efter | Swedenborg sökte själen med skalpell | Names the person and the surprising physical act at the heart of the essay. |
| Skelettet som höll | Koranen mellan pergament och minne | Makes the subject and its two forms of transmission visible. |
| Marknaden i raden | Ska moskén tala om hur du ska rösta? | States the civic dilemma without presenting disputed endorsements as fact. |
| Spegeln som blev fönster | Vad vet du om kvinnan bakom slöjan? | Invites readers to examine what clothing can actually tell them about a person. |
| Serumet och blicken | Kallocain: sanningen under tvång | Names the literary work and the conflict that drives the comparison. |
| Chipet och riddjuret | Att uppgradera en människa | Gives the body-optimization debate an accessible, concrete entry point. |
| Ormboet i hjärtat | När någon annans lycka gör ont | Makes envy recognizable as a lived experience. |
| Passionens klängväxt | Ett ord räcker inte för kärlek | Introduces the essay’s distinctions between forms of love without a numerical gimmick. |
| Lögnens bokföring | Allt du måste minnas när du ljuger | Turns the cognitive burden of lying into a specific reason to read. |
| Förrådshusens väktare | Vem ser till att vi har mat? | Puts shared responsibility and food preparedness directly in view. |
| Tungans fästning | Du ansvarar också för det du inte säger | Surfaces the essay’s less obvious argument: withholding necessary speech carries responsibility. |
| Plantan vid Stunden | Plantera, även om världen går under | Uses the essay’s actual, striking image to introduce action beyond expected reward. |

This is an editorial review, not a fact-check of essay claims.
