# Feature images

Every essay, essäserie and Fördjupning page opens on a photograph made for the site, and
the same picture is its card on the front page and the hubs. They are made with Codex's
image tool. The user is a photographer and judges each one by eye; every rule below comes
from their verdicts, so treat it as a decision, not a taste.

**Nothing is placed before the user has chosen it. Never commit or deploy unasked.**

## ⭐ What the user wants

On 2026-11-07 I proposed 23 replacements for 18 pictures on the front page and the hubs:
conceptual, deadpan frames in flat Nordic daylight, no figure from behind, no lamp glow, a
symbol to decode in each. The user kept 15 of the originals — »the current is the clear
winner since it is much more intelligent and emotional and a clear master piece« — and
said of mine: »waaay to boring, lack emotion and a sense of artistic style«. What the
pictures they keep share is the brief:

- **A human presence carrying a feeling**: devotion, tenderness, longing, grief, awe. A
  woman praying by lamplight, a couple close in a boat, a figure under the stars, a man
  looking towards the Kaaba from a pillar's shadow. People are welcome: from behind, in
  profile, in silhouette, small in a landscape, or as a pair of hands.
- **Light as drama**: one warm source in darkness, the last light of the day, a night
  sky, snow, a storm. Lamplight, candlelight and dusk are wanted, not banned.
- **A story caught in a moment**, felt before it is understood. The painterly and Romantic
  line: Rembrandt and Vermeer interiors, Caspar David Friedrich's figures before the
  sublime. Never the deadpan or conceptual line (typologies, overhead patterns, objects
  standing for an idea): the user reads it as clever and cold.
- **The register they chose** when one picture was made three ways (Döden, 2026-11-07):
  the sublime — a funeral prayer in a snowy Swedish cemetery at the last light, a row of
  small dark figures before the bier — over a lamplit deathbed and a documentary moment.

- **Muslim life in Sweden now, with people in it.** In the round after (five cards, two
  versions each) the user took the storm on the rock for Jihad, the schoolyard boys at dusk
  (wallah) for Uttryck, the men at the taps in a mosque's washing room for Tvagning and the
  father showing his son the new crescent from a suburban balcony for Islamiskt nyår. They
  passed over both historical monuments (Samarra's minaret, the Umayyad courtyard) and
  kept Kalifatet's old picture rather than take either. Prefer a living scene here and now
  to a place from history.

- **On the Tro och lära cards, the family at home.** In that dark lamplit series the user
  took a grandmother and granddaughter reading the Quran at the kitchen table, and a mother
  pouring tea for her sleepy son at suhur, and kept three old cards over a convert's
  shahada among brothers, a newborn's adhan, du'a under the aurora, a farmer sowing and a
  fisherman before a storm. Bönen's midnight-sun and roadside landscapes were sent back.
  Within this series, reach first for an intimate scene in a Muslim home.

What they have rejected, each once (memory files hold the detail):

- A concept still life (an old man's hands, a house key, a phone): »en av de sämsta
  bilder jag sett«, »typiskt ai genererat«. Objects staged to stand for an idea.
- A lone person by a laptop: »för tråkig«. A picture with no feeling in it.
- A centred, everything-sharp sunset scene: »fotografiskt är detta inte alls en bra bild«.
  Composition and craft count as much as the motif.
- A staged period set (a 1600s night prayer room: moonlight, an oil lamp, a coffee pot
  on a stool): »looks too ai generated, must look more like a photographic masterpiece«.
  The same story as a present-day scene in a real place (a rooftop in Old Sana'a at blue
  hour, a man praying in silhouette) passed. Note the user does keep strong period and
  mythic pictures that hold as photographs: the Viking close-up for »Vikingarna hade inte
  ångest« and the huddled figure in a frozen waste for »Klimatångesten är ingen Ragnarök«.
- Five covers of one scene in a row (a person at a desk by a window, lamp against dusk):
  »they all three almost look the same seen together making it feel like they are AI
  generated«. The fault was the sameness, not the lamplight.
- A crop no photographer would make: »the way that is cut or cropped does not look like
  the thinking of a master photographer«.

## The gates — every picture

- **Modesty.** No woman's hair, anywhere in the frame, background figures included; a
  woman wears a hijab that covers all of it. Faces turned away, in shadow or out of frame.
- **The faith shown right.** A mushaf, not a leather Bible with gilt edges. Prayer rows
  straight and closed; at a funeral prayer the rows, then the imam alone, then the bier,
  everyone facing it (the user caught a bier standing in a gap in the row). Ritual dress
  and gestures as they are done. No crosses, no generated script or calligraphy (it comes
  out as gibberish), no Shia or Sufi ritual.
- **The qibla.** From Sweden Mecca lies to the south-east. Anyone praying faces that way,
  and the light must agree: the midnight sun is due north, a winter sunset south-west. A
  man praying towards the midnight sun was facing away from Mecca (Bönen, 2026-11-07); the
  edit turned him to face the camera with the sun behind him.
- **A photograph, not a render.** Film grain, real skin and cloth, imperfect surfaces.
  No plastic skin, HDR, over-smoothing, teal-and-orange grade.
- **Hold the pictures already on the site to the same gates.** On 2026-11-07 the lamplit
  night prayer the user had kept since October had a small cross on the wall behind the
  praying woman, and Änglarna and Förutbestämmelsen carried generated pseudo-Arabic. Zoom
  into every picture in a set you review, kept ones included; fix by edit.
- **Clean.** No text, logo or watermark; look at the bottom-right corner at full size for
  the generator's sparkle mark (one of six carried it in October).

## How

### 1 · Read the text for its feeling

Read the description and the sections. Ask: who is in this, what do they feel, what
moment is it, where does the light come from? Döden became a funeral prayer at dusk, not
a symbol of death; Resenären som sitter still became a fig tree in a snowy window (the
old country growing in the new), which the user took.

### 2 · Look at the row it will stand in

A picture is seen with its neighbours: the front page's three-card rows, the /essaer/ tab
trios, the /fordjupning/ grid, the seven /tradar/ covers. Lay the neighbours out at card
size (373×249) before briefing, and make this one a different scene in the same register.
Döden's snow cemetery now stands beside Muslimsk begravning's graves in snow; the user
chose it anyway, but say so when it happens.

### 3 · Brief, with their pictures attached

```bash
S=.claude/skills/feature-image/scripts
$S/bild.sh refs <run>          # the approved pictures as PNG references in the run folder
# write <run>/<name>.txt for each version (skeleton below)
$S/bild.sh gen <run> <name> …  # Codex, references attached, five at a time
$S/bild.sh sheet <run> <name> …  # card-size contact sheet and a corner sheet
```

Make two versions of each picture, in two registers (the sublime, and an intimate or
documentary moment), so the user chooses between real alternatives. For a new kind of
subject, calibrate first: one picture in three registers, the user picks, then the batch.

The skeleton that worked (2026-11-07):

> Create exactly one image with your image generation tool, then stop and reply with one
> short line.
>
> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a single landscape 3:2 photograph (generate at
> 1536x1024) for the page '<title>' on a serious Swedish publication on Islam. <One or two
> sentences on what the text argues.>
>
> The attached photographs were published by this publication and its editor considers
> them masterpieces. Match their emotional depth, their painterly light (one source or the
> last light of the day, deep soft shadow, warm against cool), their rich but natural
> colour, their finish and their sense of a real moment. Do not copy their subjects or
> compositions. The picture must move the viewer before it explains anything: a human
> presence carrying a feeling, light as drama, a story caught in a moment, in the spirit
> of Rembrandt and Vermeer interiors and of Caspar David Friedrich's figures before the
> sublime, but a true photograph by a great photographer, not a painting and not a film
> still. Every figure whole inside the frame unless the brief crops it on purpose.
>
> Register: <the sublime | intimate, interior, chiaroscuro | a humanist moment>. <The
> scene: who, doing what, feeling what, where the light falls, where in the frame.>
>
> Real photographic texture: fine film grain, true skin and fabric, imperfect real
> surfaces, natural depth of field. No plastic skin, no HDR, no over-smoothing, no
> teal-and-orange grade. No readable text, letters or
> script anywhere, no logos, no watermark, no border, no generator mark. Faces turned
> away, in shadow or out of frame; no woman with visible hair (a woman wears a hijab
> covering all her hair). No weapons, no crosses.

### 4 · Judge it as a photograph, then in its row

At full size: the gates above, hands and fingers, the rite. At card size in its row: does
it hold, does it repeat a neighbour, does any edge cut a figure or an object where no
photographer would? Codex never lands edges; when the frame needs it, crop by measurement
(a brightness profile finds rows, gaps and margins).

**Fix by edit, not by rerun.** `bild.sh edit` changes one thing and keeps the picture the
user chose: the Bible-looking book became a mushaf, the bier moved out of the row. A
fresh run with the corrected brief got the rite right and lost the look.

### 5 · The user chooses

Put the current picture beside the versions on a review page (an Artifact with the `db`
capability, picks in a collection; the 2026-11-07 page is
https://claude.ai/artifact/PYAkFwRBpu1BqENYVKNrv7 and its builder is
`~/.cache/islam-se-bilder/granskning/build.py`). Show the rows as they would read. Offer
»keep current« honestly: the user kept 15 of 18 originals over my proposals once. Read the
notes as well as the picks; the note is where the direction comes from.

### 6 · Place

| What | File | Text fields |
|---|---|---|
| Essay | `apps/web/src/assets/images/<slug>.webp` (1536×1024 or 1672×941) | `imageAlt`, `imageCaption` in `data/articles/<slug>.md` |
| Fördjupning | `apps/web/src/assets/images/fordjupning/<slug>.webp` (1536×1024) | `imageAlt` in `data/fordjupning/<slug>.md` |
| Essäserie | `apps/web/src/assets/images/tradar/<id>.webp` (1536×1024) | `imageAlt` in `apps/web/src/content/tradar/tradar.json` |

- WebP quality 84, `-alpha off -strip` (Codex PNGs carry alpha). The file is the whole wiring.
- An essay's `<slug>-mobile.webp` (750×937) is shown only when it is landscape
  (`EssayImage.astro`); recut it from the new picture so the pair stays one picture.
- The front page's lead crops the essay picture to no flatter than 5:2; keep the subject
  in the central band. Every essay picture gets the site's tonal wash in CSS.
- **Alt text** says what is seen, for someone who cannot see it; no » « and no double
  quotes. **Caption**: one or two short sentences in the essay's voice, no first person
  (»Morgonen efter allhelgona. Ljusen har brunnit ut.«).
- Not here: the Tro och lära cards (`images/fakta/`), which the user keeps in their own
  lamplit made series, and documentary photographs, which are cut from the original and
  credited (`imageDocumentary`, DESIGN.md).

Check with `pnpm -w lint:house`, then look at the page and its row on the dev server.

## Codex

- `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -s read-only -C <dir> -i a.png -i b.png - < brief.txt`:
  `-i` swallows a trailing prompt, so the prompt goes on stdin with `-`, which also closes
  stdin (an open stdin hangs forever). The picture lands in
  `~/.codex/generated_images/<session id>/`; `bild.sh` copies it out.
- The built-in tool runs on the user's ChatGPT plan and gives 1536×1024 at most. Explicit
  `gpt-image-2` with `quality high` and 2048×1152 or 3840×2160 is Codex's CLI fallback and
  needs an `OPENAI_API_KEY`, billed per image; none was set on 2026-11-07. Offer it for a
  final picture that must be larger, never switch silently.
- Ask for landscape outright: one of ten came back upright.
- `/tmp` is a tmpfs that fills (other projects' caches); work in
  `~/.cache/islam-se-bilder/` (`BILD_WORK`), never in `/tmp`.
